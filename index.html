League Info

About the League

Format

The Wildwood Men's Golf League plays every Thursday evening at Wildwood Country Club in Burlington, ND. Each week teams play a 9-hole scramble, alternating between the front nine (holes 1–9) and back nine (holes 10–18).

Scramble Rules

  1. All team members tee off on each hole.
  2. The team selects the best drive and all players hit from that spot.
  3. This continues until the ball is holed — always playing from the best shot.
  4. Each player must contribute at least 2 drives per 9-hole round.
  5. For putting, all team members putt from the selected spot; the ball must be placed within one putter-head length but no closer to the hole.
  6. Maximum score per hole is double par.

Money Holes & Coupon Vouchers

Each week, 9 money holes are randomly assigned across the nine played:

  • 3 Longest Drive holes — longest drive in the fairway wins a pro shop coupon voucher.
  • 3 Longest Putt holes — longest holed putt wins a coupon voucher.
  • 3 Closest to the Pin holes — closest tee shot (1st or 2nd shot depending on hole) wins a coupon voucher.

Money holes are announced before each round. Winners are tracked on the scorecard and posted here after the round.

Beat the Pro Shop

The pro shop sets a target score each week. If your team matches or beats that score, you beat the pro shop! Results are posted weekly alongside the leaderboard.

Team Sizes

  • Teams consist of 2–4 players.
  • A minimum of 2 players is required to post a valid scramble score.
  • Teams with fewer than the required minimum may be combined at the discretion of the league coordinator.
