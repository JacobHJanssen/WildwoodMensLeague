Wildwood Men's Golf League

About the League

What Is It?

The Wildwood Men's Golf League is a weekly 9-hole scramble competition open to golfers of all skill levels at Wildwood Country Club in Burlington, ND. Games are played every Thursday throughout the season, with teams alternating between the front nine and back nine from week to week.

Results are posted each Friday morning by league manager Jake Janssen. The league is designed to be fun, competitive, and social — no serious handicap requirements, just get out and play.

Scramble Format Rules

  1. Each team consists of 4 players.
  2. All four players tee off on every hole.
  3. The team selects the best tee shot and all players move their ball to that spot.
  4. All four players hit their next shot from the selected spot.
  5. This continues until the ball is holed — always playing from the best shot each time.
  6. One team score is recorded per hole. No individual scores are tracked.
  7. The team's final score is the total of all 9 hole scores compared to par.

Mini-Game Contests

Each Thursday, Jake randomly draws a hole assignment for each of the four mini-game contests and posts them on the pro shop board before play begins. Check the board when you arrive.

  • 🏌️ Longest Drive — Team's longest tee shot on the assigned hole.
  • 🐍 Longest Putt — Longest putt made on the assigned hole.
  • 🎯 Closest to Pin (1st Shot) — Closest approach on the first shot on the assigned hole.
  • 📍 Closest to Pin (2nd Shot) — Closest approach on the second shot on the assigned hole.

Record your results on the scorecard submission form by end of day Thursday.

Team Requirements

  • Teams must have exactly 4 players.
  • Maximum team size: 4 players (no substitutes mid-season without approval from Jake).
  • Open to all men 18 and older. No handicap requirement.
  • Teams must be registered before the season begins.
  • All four players must tee off on every hole — no two-man or three-man scramble allowed.

Scheduling

  • Play is every Thursday. Teams may tee off any time Thursday.
  • Scorecards must be submitted (via this website or in person at the pro shop) by end of day Thursday.
  • The nine played alternates weekly: Week 1 = Front Nine, Week 2 = Back Nine, and so on.
  • The full 18-hole course remains open to all golfers — only your 9-hole scoring window changes.
  • Results are posted Friday morning on this website by Jake Janssen.
